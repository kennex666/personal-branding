## 🧠 Overview

**1Box Studios Logo** is an original digital artwork created by **Duong Thai Bao** as the primary brand identifier for **1Box Studios**.

The logo encapsulates the identity of the studio, designed for versatility across multiple mediums, platforms, and thematic environments. 

This specific repository/folder acts as the canonical collection of the 1Box Studios logo variations, documenting its complete visual identity suite:
- **Full Variants**: Primary logos including typography and symbols, optimized for main branding and banners (`logo-color-bg-full`, `logo-color-gradient-full`, `logo-dev-color-gradient-full`, `logo-gray-bg-full`).
- **Short Variants**: Compact icon-only logos optimized for avatars, favicons, and space-constrained environments (`logo-color-short`, `logo-dark-short`, `logo-dark-bg-short`).
